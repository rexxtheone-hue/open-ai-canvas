# Festive campaign

A matching set for one festival or sale: product visuals, an ad image with the offer, and a short video, all in one look.

## 1. Questions

- Which festival or sale? (detect from the date or wording if obvious)
- What to make? `[Full set: 3 images + 1 video / Images only / Video only]`
- Offer text and greeting, for example "Gong Xi Fa Cai, 20% off until 15 Feb".
- Language? `[Mandarin / Malay / English / Bilingual]`

## 2. Festival look (respectful and accurate)

| Festival | Visual cues | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Chinese New Year | red and gold, mandarin oranges, lanterns, ang pao, plum blossom | black and white palettes, the number 4 |
| Hari Raya Aidilfitri | green and gold, ketupat, pelita oil lamps, songkok and baju kurung, open house | alcohol, pork, revealing outfits, non-halal food |
| Deepavali | oil lamps (diya), kolam patterns, marigold, rich purples, oranges and gold | shoes on a kolam, religious idols as decoration |
| Christmas | warm lights, pine, gift boxes, tropical Malaysian Christmas works well | snow-heavy scenes unless asked |
| Merdeka and Malaysia Day | Jalur Gemilang colours, hibiscus, unity of all races | distorted or misused flags |
| 11.11, 12.12, payday | bold sale typography, countdown feel, bright brand colours | festive religious symbols |

## 3. Plan and generate

1. Hero product image (`/product-photoshoot` style): product unchanged, festival setting from the table, 4:5.
2. Offer ad: same scene, headline and offer text quoted exactly, 4:5 or 1:1.
3. Story image: 9:16 version with the greeting.
4. Video (8–15s, 9:16): image to video from the hero image, gentle camera move, festive ambient sound and the greeting spoken or shown, product stays identical.
Show the plan and total price first.

## 4. Deliver

Present the set together with suggested posting order (teaser, offer, last-day reminder). Offer the same set for the next festival or in another language.
