# Drama clip formula

## From scenes to clips

- One clip plays one place, a whole number of seconds within the model's range (Seedance 2.5: 4–30 s; aim for 15–30). A change of place is always a new clip.
- Fill a clip with consecutive beats of one place. Split a long scene only right after a completed line, on the listener's reaction; the next clip opens on the other person with the same positions, postures and held props.
- A location's first appearance opens with 1–2 s and no line; an enclosed room is never empty at the first frame (someone already moving or entering within a second).
- The last line ends about 2 s before the clip ends; those seconds play the next action or reaction. A cliffhanger ends on a slow push-in on the face whose decision is open, then cut before anyone answers.
- References per clip, in order: the location picture, each character sheet seen, each prop that matters (the product photo for the product). Never attach someone who is only heard.

## Shot design

- Most shots 1–2.5 s; quick 0.8–1.2 s cuts only at a peak. A clip of up to 15 s has at most 15 shots. The longest shot is on the turn.
- One readable event and at most two actions per shot. A speaker's mouth stays free while they talk (no eating or drinking in their line).
- Sizes: full, medium-wide, medium, medium-close, close, extreme-close. A face carrying a line is close enough to read (name the crop: "cut at the collarbones"). Never more than 5 s without a close shot. Consecutive shots of the same person change angle and jump two sizes.
- Positions by screen side and depth (near, middle, far), kept across cuts; distances stated (arm's length, three steps). Eyelines never at the camera; the person looked at is off the side the eyes go. Height follows power: slightly low on who presses, slightly high on who loses ground.
- Camera: never zoom. Handheld in heated exchanges, slow push-in when a line lands, pull-out on a shock, travel with a moving body.
- 9:16 vertical: stage in depth and height, faces centred, separate two people in depth not sideways, no pans or sideways tracks. 16:9: side-by-side two-shots and pans allowed.
- Every shot line opens in motion and names what each body does and who else is visible and where. Never name a feeling, a sigh, a gasp, a blush, glowing skin, wet eyes or readable text.

## Speech

- Window per line: words / 3 seconds, plus at most 0.5 s (Chinese: characters / 4). A spare second gets filled with invented words, so plan tight.
- The next line starts 0.1–0.3 s after the previous one, about 1 s after a written reaction, 1–2 s after an action.
- Every spoken line is quoted exactly once, in the clip that plays it. A delivery note never shares a word with its line.
- Voice cards: write each speaker's card on their first line in the clip, word for word the same in every clip; it opens with sex and age ("a man's voice of about 45, warm baritone").
- Thoughts are the character's voice-over with lips closed. An unnamed narrator has one fixed voice for the whole film.
- No on-screen text of any kind (no captions or title cards) unless a prop carries it.

## Clip prompt (English, each part on its own line, in this order)

1. Sound rule: "STRICT RULE: Absolutely no music, only diegetic sounds." (With no line in the clip add "; no one speaks." With a narrator: "…and the narrator's voice-over.")
2. Style line: "Realism cinematic, real skin with pores and small marks, natural eyes, people and set lit only by this place's named sources: [the key source, where it is, how it falls, what stays dark]; [contrast and palette]; [the air]." Copy the same sentence into every clip of the scene.
3. Look line: "Framed on the rule of thirds, with a light anamorphic lens character and a fine 35mm film grain."
4. Reference lines, one per attached picture in order: "Image reference 1 (location Kitchen): the Kitchen 100% as in the reference." "Image reference 2 (character Mei): Mei, face, hair, body and clothes 100% as in the reference." "Image reference 4 (product): the product 100% as in the reference, same label." Then "Continuity: [each person's current state, e.g. Mei is clean and unchanged from the reference]."
5. Cast and geography: "In frame only: Mei and Ahmad. [Landmarks.] [Who stands where, by side and depth, how far apart.] Eyelines never at the camera."
6. Opening state: everyone at the first frame, each stance in one word, distances.
7. Speech budgets, one per speaker: "Mei speaks aloud 2 times between 1.6s and 10.3s and never again." Then who never speaks.
8. Intentions, one per acting character (≤30 words): what they want and the one drive they act with (grit, resilience, persistence, determination). No other emotion words.
9. Pace: "Pace: tight and pressing; every shot opens in motion." Never ask for stillness or pauses.
10. Shot lines: "S1 0.0-1.5s · medium_wide, from the doorway, eye level, slow push-in · [action of each body, who is where]." Sound events with their time.
11. Audio: "All speech is in [Mandarin/Malay/English]. Only the words inside double quotation marks are ever spoken aloud; every voice direction and every other word of this text stays silent." Then one line per spoken line: "S2 (1.6-3.5s) Mei to Ahmad, a woman's voice of about 30, a low clear alto, tight and quiet: "…"". Then "No speech in S1, S4."
12. Restrictions (last): "- No music of any kind. - No added sound effects (no whoosh, boom or riser). - Only diegetic sound: the characters' voices and the natural sounds of this place. - No words but the quoted lines, each only in its own window." If background people appear: "- Background people never copy the face or hair of any image reference."

## Settings

Seedance 2.5, operation reference_to_video, generate_audio on, the episode's aspect ratio, duration in whole seconds. Draft at 480p first; remake approved clips at 1080p only after the user approves the draft. A clip that continues the same scene can use the previous clip as a reference video so motion carries over.

## Check each clip

Right shape and length, sound present, same faces as the sheets, product correct. A clip that came back wrong is left out and named in one line; remake only when the user asks (show the cost).
