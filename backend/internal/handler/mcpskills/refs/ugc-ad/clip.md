# UGC clip formula (video from the storyboard)

One clip animates one storyboard: 8 hard cuts, the creator speaking the script lines, native sound.

## Settings

Seedance 2.5 (Flash for drafts), operation reference_to_video, generate_audio on, 9:16, duration = the clip's planned seconds (4–15 per clip; split longer videos into several clips, for example 30 s = 15 + 15). References in order: image 1 = storyboard, image 2 = creator image, image 3 = product photo (if any).

## Template (each part on its own line, in this order)

Style & Mood: UGC iPhone look, [light matching the board], [all selfie: front camera, intimate handheld feel | all static: locked-off phone on a counter | mixed: selfie handheld and static cuts alternate], vertical social video.

Narrative Summary: [one sentence: what happens across the 8 cuts and why], performed by a natural, engaged creator — genuine reactions, lively but human, never staged screaming. (Use "an energetic, hyped creator" only if the user asked for hype; drop the phrase entirely for calm, luxury or routine videos.)

Dynamic Description:
Cut 1 (0–Xs) — [distance] [selfie/static]: [action from frame 1; what each hand does; two small behaviours such as a hair tuck or a glance down; expression; product position]. Hard cut to.
Cut 2 … Cut 8 (Ys–end): [same pattern, each a different distance or camera from the one before].

Static Description: [1–2 sentences: the room, props, light direction, as on the board].

Audio: She speaks to camera, iPhone microphone sound with natural room tone: "[the script lines, split across the cuts at natural phrase breaks]"
(First clip only, optional: a short natural sound at the very start, written in brackets, such as [small laugh] or [soft gasp].)
(Music only if the user asked: "Music: [genre], low under the voice.")

Quality suffix (always last): "Face clear and undistorted, same clothes throughout. Shot on iPhone, natural light, social media look, [all selfie: slight natural handheld shake | all static: completely still camera, no drift | mixed: handheld shake in selfie cuts, still frame in static cuts]. No on-screen text, subtitles, captions or watermarks; no legible text except the product's own label; no real brand logos; no cinematic grade, film grain, bokeh, lens flare, fisheye, slow motion or beauty filter; no extra or deformed hands."

## Timing

Split the clip evenly across 8 cuts, with the hook cut a little shorter (about 1.5 s) and the call-to-action cut a little longer. Speech must fit: about 2.5 English words, 4 Chinese characters or 2 Malay words per second of spoken time. The first word is heard within the first half-second.

## Action language

Describe each cut by what moves: "twists the cap off with her right hand while the left holds the phone", "tilts the bottle toward the lens", "presses a pea-sized amount onto her fingertip". One product action per cut. Name the exact body part (left cheek, back of the hand). A cap that comes off stays off in later cuts.

## Continuing into a second clip

The second clip's board uses the first board as image 3. Its speech continues mid-thought: no greeting, no re-introducing the product, never repeat a line from the first clip.

## Never

A line repeated in two cuts; "hey guys", "okay so", "stop scrolling" openers; invented results, reviews, sales numbers or before/after claims (see `script`); a cut where both hands hold objects in a selfie.
