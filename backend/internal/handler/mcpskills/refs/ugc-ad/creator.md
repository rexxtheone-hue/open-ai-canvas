# Creator image formula

The creator image is the identity every later picture and clip copies. Make it once per video (or reuse the user's photo or saved character) and never regenerate it mid-run.

## Rules

- It must look like a real creator's phone selfie, not a photo shoot. This matters more than anything else.
- No product in this image. The product enters later in the storyboard.
- Adults only (21+ appearance). Never a real or famous person; a user's own photo only if it is theirs or they have consent.
- Cast for the product and the buyer: a skincare brand for working women in KL casts a woman in her late 20s; a car accessory casts someone believable next to a car. Malaysian casting by default: Chinese, Malay, Indian or mixed Malaysian, matching the brand's audience; describe features, skin tone and hair, never write the ethnicity word itself unless the user asked.
- One bold visual anchor only (a striking hair colour, statement earrings, a bright scarf, a tudung in a strong colour); everything else stays quiet around it.
- Modest, current outfits by default: tops that close at the collarbone, regular fit. Respect tudung and modest wear when the audience calls for it.
- Cool or neutral daylight on the face. Never golden hour, sunset or orange cast (it turns skin waxy in video).

## Template (fill the brackets, keep the rest)

"A spontaneous iPhone selfie of a [age band] [woman/man], [one expression from the list below], [hair: colour, length, texture, styling — the bold anchor lives here if it is hair], [build], with attractive, well-proportioned features and natural skin texture, standing in [a specific room or place with 2–3 architectural details]. [Light source and direction] falls across the face, clean and neutral, no warm cast; real skin with visible pores and small unevenness. [She/He] wears [outfit: each garment with colour, fabric and fit, closed at the collarbone, plus one accessory]. Body relaxed and neutral, one hand resting naturally. The background shows [materials, colours, furniture]. Colour palette of [2–3 tones, neutrals first]. Self-portrait shot on an iPhone front camera at arm's length, head and shoulders fill the frame, slight natural tilt, slightly off-centre, captured mid-moment, not posed. Phone sensor grain and real skin texture kept, no retouching, no smoothing filter. No fisheye, no ultra-wide distortion. An authentic creator selfie, not an editorial portrait, not a fashion magazine shot."

Expressions (pick one, never "warm smile at the camera"): mid-thought with a slight half-smile, eyes just off the lens · caught mid-laugh, head slightly tilted · glancing toward the lens with a relaxed neutral face · looking up from her phone, unguarded · in the middle of saying something · wide-eyed at the start of a reaction, brows lifted · playful mock-shock with a grin breaking through · eyebrows raised mid-thought, lips pressed in a "wait, no" line.

Never write: editorial, portrait (as a genre), poised, elegant pose, flawless or glowing skin, ring light, studio strobe, centred composition, straight-on, posing.

## Model and settings

An image model that renders realistic people well (Nano Banana or GPT Image class; Seedream for East Asian casting also works), 3:4, 2k. No reference images unless the user supplied the person.

## Malaysian setting ideas

Condo bedroom with a window to the city and a standing fan · terrace-house kitchen with tiled backsplash · office pantry · car driver's seat with seatbelt on, parked · kopitiam table · bathroom with mosaic tiles · mall corridor with soft overhead light.
