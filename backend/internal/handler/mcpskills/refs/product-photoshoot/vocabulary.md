# Photography vocabulary, text rules and AVOID lists

Vague words make average pictures. Replace every vague word with a concrete one from this sheet.

## Light

Direction: 45 degrees camera-left or camera-right (classic key), frontal flat (e-commerce, beauty), overhead soft box, three-quarter back rim (separation), low side raking light (texture), butterfly from above (glamour), window light from one side.
Quality: large diffused softbox, hard direct strobe, narrow gridded spot, bounced fill, open overcast sky, dappled leaf shadow, a practical lamp visible in frame.
Colour temperature, always as a number: candle 2000–2800K, warm home lamp 3000–3400K, lived-in interior 3500–4000K, neutral interior 4000–4500K, studio daylight 5000–5500K, overcast 6000–6500K, shade or blue hour 7000K+. Malaysian midday sun is hard and high: say "hard overhead tropical sun, short shadows" when outdoors at noon.
Shadows: soft gradient falloff, hard-edged shadow, contact shadow only at the base, dappled broken shadow, deep shadow side with a little fill, gentle wraparound.

## Lens

24mm wide (environment, slight stretch), 35mm (documentary, lifestyle), 50mm (natural eye), 85mm (flattering portrait compression), 100mm macro (detail), 135mm (strong isolation).
Aperture: f/1.4–f/2 dreamy and shallow, f/2.8 classic portrait, f/4–f/5.6 subject and context both readable, f/8–f/11 everything sharp (catalog).

## Surfaces and texture

Seamless paper, linen, raw oak or walnut, polished marble, terrazzo, concrete, brushed metal, velvet, worn leather, glazed ceramic, woven rattan, batik cloth, songket weave, banana leaf, kopitiam marble with a metal rim. Texture qualifiers: tack-sharp surface detail, visible skin pores, individual hair strands, condensation droplets on glass, dust catching light, real fabric weave.

## Palettes (always name 2–3 tones)

Warm earth (terracotta, ochre, dried sage) · cool neutral (bone white, soft grey, deep navy) · festive red and gold · Raya green and gold · pastel trio (dusty rose, sage, butter) · black with one saturated red accent · tropical fresh (leaf green, coconut white, sky blue).

## Quality phrases (end of every prompt)

"tack-sharp commercial photography, hyper-detailed, ultra-realistic materials, editorial commercial standard". Never write "good lighting", "nice background", "beautiful", "professional photo", "high quality" or "modern style": name the light, the surface, the angle, the lens and the palette instead.

## Text in the image: three cases

1. The user gave exact words: put them in the prompt in quotes with the font style, position and contrast: "Integrated headline reads "RM39 SAHAJA" in heavy condensed sans-serif, white on the deep red band at the top." Quote Chinese and Malay text exactly. Prefer GPT Image or Nano Banana class models for text.
2. The user will add text later themselves: ask for "one calm, tonally even area that is still part of the scene (sky, soft wall, blurred background)". Never say "empty space" or "negative space for text": models draw a flat band.
3. Nothing was said about text: do not mention text at all.

## AVOID lists (assemble one block, no repeats)

Universal (always): no AI artefacts, no warped or invented text, no plastic or waxy surfaces, no extra fingers or limbs, no melted geometry, no duplicated product, no over-saturated HDR or halos, no over-sharpening, no flat fluorescent light, no harsh on-camera flash, no stock-photo poses, no unrelated logos, no watermarks.
People (hands, faces): no uncanny faces, no asymmetric eyes, no warped hands, no over-smoothed or orange skin, no doll eyes, no stiff posture, no warped glasses or jewellery.
Labels (product has text): no garbled letters, no fake brand names, no doubled or melted labels.
Stock feel (lifestyle, hero, ads): no over-staged scene, no unrealistically clean room, no perfect symmetry, no obviously pasted background.
Restyle: commit fully to the new style, no half-applied transformation.
